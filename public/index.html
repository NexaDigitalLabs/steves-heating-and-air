Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about HVAC service in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service?
Yes — same-day emergency response for heating and cooling failures across Murrieta, Temecula, and Southwest Riverside County. If your AC is out in summer heat or your furnace died on a cold night, call (951) 634-3233 and we'll get someone out to you as fast as possible.
Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?
Yes. Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating is fully Licensed, Bonded, and Insured. Our technicians are also NATE-certified (North American Technician Excellence) — a nationally recognized HVAC certification. Every job, every tech, fully covered.
Do you give free estimates?
Yes — free estimates on all installations and replacements. A service-call diagnostic fee may apply for repairs. Call (951) 634-3233 for current rates and to schedule your free quote.
What HVAC brands do you service?
All major HVAC brands — including Carrier, Lennox, Trane, York, Rheem, Goodman, Daikin, American Standard, Bryant, and Amana. If you have a different brand, call us and ask — we likely service it.
What areas do you cover?
We serve all of Southwest Riverside County: Murrieta, Temecula, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Canyon Lake, Menifee, Sun City, Winchester, French Valley, and Perris. If you're outside that list, call and ask — we'll let you know straight away.
How long have you been in business?
Over 20 years serving the Murrieta-Temecula Valley and Southwest Riverside County. American-owned, locally operated, family-run.
How often should I have my HVAC serviced?
Once a year minimum — ideally a spring AC tune-up and a fall furnace inspection. Annual maintenance catches small problems before they become emergency failures and keeps your system running efficiently. Skipping it can cut equipment lifespan dramatically.
Should I repair or replace my old HVAC unit?
Call us for an honest assessment. As a rule of thumb: if your system is over 12-15 years old and the repair costs more than half the price of a new unit, replacement usually makes more sense. We'll lay out both options with real numbers — no upsell pressure, no high-pressure sales.
How long does a new HVAC system last?
With proper maintenance, central AC units typically last 15-20 years, furnaces 15-25 years, and heat pumps 12-15 years. Skipping annual maintenance can cut those numbers dramatically.
Do you offer energy audits?
Yes. Our comprehensive energy audit identifies exactly where your home or business is losing energy: poorly sealed ductwork, insufficient insulation, drafty windows, oversized or undersized HVAC equipment, and thermal weak spots. You get a written report with prioritized recommendations and projected savings for each fix. Most contractors don't offer this service — we do because it genuinely saves customers money.
