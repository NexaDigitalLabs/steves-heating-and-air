Your Local French Valley HVAC Contractor — New Construction and Acreage

Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating has been the trusted HVAC contractor for French Valley homeowners for over 20 years. We're a locally-owned Murrieta-based company. When you call (951) 634-3233, you reach a real local team that knows the difference between French Valley's newer subdivision homes and the older rural properties along the airport boundary.

French Valley is an unincorporated community sitting just north of Murrieta and east of Temecula. It's seen significant residential growth over the past 15-20 years, with master-planned subdivisions building out along Winchester Road and Briggs Road. North of these, the housing transitions to larger rural and equestrian parcels stretching toward Winchester. The summer heat here mirrors Murrieta — frequent 100°F+ afternoons.

We service every part of French Valley — the newer subdivisions with modern variable-speed equipment, the rural acreage with mixed equipment ages, and the homes near the airport with their own quirks around noise and air quality from runway proximity. Same-day response is our standard, typically within 1-3 hours.

French Valley HVAC Questions

Local answers about HVAC service in French Valley, CA. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

Does Steve's serve all of French Valley?
All of French Valley — the master-planned subdivisions along Winchester and Briggs, the airport area, and the rural properties stretching north. Same-day response from Murrieta, typically 1-3 hours.
How fast can you respond to an HVAC emergency in French Valley?
For most addresses in this area we have a NATE-certified tech on-site within 1-3 hours during business hours. We're locally based in Murrieta. Call (951) 634-3233 for fastest response.
Do you service newer construction with variable-speed equipment?
Yes — most newer French Valley homes have modulating or two-stage equipment that needs proper commissioning to actually perform as designed. We tune airflow, verify refrigerant charge to spec, and confirm thermostat programming. Manufacturer specs often aren't met by the install crew, and that costs you efficiency for the life of the equipment.
Do you handle horse properties and acreage north of the airport?
Yes — rural and equestrian properties are a regular part of our French Valley work. We're set up for long driveways, separate barn systems, and equipment positioning that works around property layout and livestock considerations.
What HVAC services do you offer in French Valley?
Everything HVAC: AC repair and installation, furnace repair and replacement, heat pumps, residential and commercial refrigeration, water heaters (tank, tankless, hybrid), indoor air quality systems (HRV, MERV-13+, UV), HVAC maintenance and tune-ups, comprehensive home energy audits, and full ductwork retrofits.
Are you licensed to work in French Valley?
Yes — fully Licensed, Bonded, and Insured. NATE-certified technicians (North American Technician Excellence). California CSLB License #1031139. We've been the trusted HVAC contractor for French Valley homeowners for over 20 years.
