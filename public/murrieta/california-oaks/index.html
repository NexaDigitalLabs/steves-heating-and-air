Local HVAC Specialists for California Oaks

California Oaks is one of Murrieta's most established neighborhoods — most homes here were built between 1990 and 2002, which means a lot of original HVAC equipment is now 25-35 years old and well past its expected service life. Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating has been the trusted contractor in California Oaks for over 20 years, and we know exactly what to look for in homes from this era.

The most common California Oaks issues we see: original 80% AFUE furnaces struggling through January, AC condensers failing on the original R-22 refrigerant (no longer manufactured, expensive to recharge), and original ductwork with significant leakage that's costing homeowners 20-30% of their conditioned air. California Oaks HVAC Questions

Local answers about HVAC service in California Oaks. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

How fast can you respond to a California Oaks HVAC emergency?
For California Oaks addresses, we typically have a NATE-certified tech on-site within 1-3 hours during business hours. We're locally based right here in Murrieta — California Oaks is one of our fastest-response areas. Call (951) 634-3233 for fastest service.
Should I replace my original 1990s California Oaks HVAC system?
If your equipment is original to a 1990s-built California Oaks home, it's almost certainly past its service life. Original AC units run on R-22 refrigerant which is no longer manufactured and increasingly expensive to top off. Original 80% AFUE furnaces are 15-20% less efficient than modern 95-96% units. We can run a no-cost assessment, including a Manual J load calculation, to right-size a replacement and project the energy savings before you commit.
Do California Oaks homes have ductwork issues?
Often, yes. Original ductwork in many California Oaks homes is now 25-35 years old, with mastic seals that have dried out and metal joints that have separated. Typical leakage in untouched 1990s ductwork runs 20-30%, which means a quarter of your conditioned air is heating or cooling your attic, not your home. We test and seal ductwork as part of any major HVAC retrofit.
Are you licensed to work in California Oaks?
Yes — fully Licensed, Bonded, and Insured. NATE-certified technicians (North American Technician Excellence). California CSLB License #1031139. We've been the trusted HVAC contractor for California Oaks homeowners for over 20 years.
Can you handle full HVAC retrofits in California Oaks?
Absolutely. Full retrofits — new AC + furnace + thermostat + ductwork — are a significant portion of our California Oaks work. We can stage the work in phases (most-urgent first) to fit your budget, or do it all at once. Manual J calculations, permit pulling, and HERS testing are all included.
