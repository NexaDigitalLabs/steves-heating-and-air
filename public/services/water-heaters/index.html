Water Heaters FAQs

Straight answers about water heaters in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

Tank vs tankless water heater — which is better for my home?
Depends on usage. Large families (4+) with high simultaneous hot water demand often do better with a tank heater or a high-capacity tankless. Smaller households or homes that want endless hot water for long showers/tubs benefit most from tankless. Tankless costs more upfront but lasts longer (~20 years vs ~10 for tank) and uses less energy. We'll run the numbers for your situation.
How much does a tankless water heater cost installed?
A typical tankless installation in our area runs $3,000-$5,500 including the unit, gas line upgrade if needed, venting, and labor. Replacing an existing tank with a same-fuel tankless is at the lower end; converting from electric to gas tankless is at the higher end. We'll give you a written quote with all costs broken out.
How long do water heaters last?
Tank water heaters: 8-12 years typical (less in our hard-water area), 12-15 years with annual flushing. Tankless: 20+ years with annual descaling. Heat pump hybrid: 10-15 years. Annual maintenance — flushing, anode rod replacement on tanks — extends life dramatically.
What's a heat pump (hybrid) water heater?
It's a water heater that uses heat pump technology (the same principle as a modern HVAC heat pump) to extract heat from surrounding air and use it to heat water. Result: 50-60% lower energy cost compared to a standard electric water heater. They cost more upfront but qualify for federal tax credits and pay back fast in California's high-cost electric market.
Are there rebates for tankless and heat pump water heaters?
Yes. SoCalGas offers rebates for qualifying gas tankless installations. The federal Inflation Reduction Act offers tax credits for heat pump water heaters. California's TECH Clean California program offers heat pump water heater rebates, though some Southern California funding is currently waitlisted — we'll check availability for your situation. Call (951) 634-3233 for current incentives.
Do you offer same-day water heater repair?
Yes — leaking tanks and water heater failures are emergencies. We provide same-day response for water heater issues across Murrieta, Temecula, and SW Riverside County. If your tank is leaking, shut off the water supply to the heater and call (951) 634-3233 immediately to prevent water damage.
What are the warning signs my water heater is failing?
Rust-colored hot water, popping/rumbling noises (sediment buildup), lukewarm water that won't get hot, water pooling around the base of the tank, age over 10 years. If you see any of these in a tank-style heater, call us before it fails completely — replacement on your timeline is much cheaper than emergency replacement plus water damage.

Yes — free estimates on all water heater installations and replacements. A diagnostic fee may apply for repair calls. Call (951) 634-3233 to schedule.
