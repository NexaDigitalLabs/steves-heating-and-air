Whole House Fans

Installation, replacement & repair of quiet, energy-saving systems

The Temecula Valley has one of the best climates in the country for a whole house fan. We get hot afternoons — but the dry inland air cools off hard at night, often dropping into the 60s even after a 100°F day. A whole house fan is built to take advantage of exactly that. When the outside air cools in the evening, you open a few windows, switch on the fan, and within 10 to 15 minutes it has pulled all that cool air through the house and pushed the trapped heat out through your attic. Your home cools down, your attic cools down, and your AC gets to rest.

A whole house fan doesn't replace your air conditioner — it works alongside it. The fan handles cool mornings, evenings, and milder days; the AC handles the hottest afternoon hours. Used together, your AC runs fewer hours, costs less to operate, and lasts longer before it needs replacing. We install modern variable-speed systems that run quietly — a soft rush of air, not the roar of the old attic fans people remember. Correct sizing and a clean, sealed installation are what keep them quiet and efficient, and that's the part we get right. If a whole house fan isn't a good fit for your home's attic or layout, we'll tell you straight.

Common Questions

Whole House Fans FAQs

Straight answers about whole house fans in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

What is a whole house fan and how does it work?
A whole house fan is a large fan mounted in your ceiling that pulls cool outside air in through open windows and pushes hot air out through your attic vents. When the evening air cools down — which it reliably does in the Temecula Valley — you open a few windows, turn on the fan, and it flushes the day's heat out of the house and attic in 10 to 15 minutes. It's not a replacement for AC, but it lets you run your AC far less.
How much can a whole house fan save on my energy bill?
It varies by home, but most Murrieta and Temecula homeowners see a noticeable drop in summer cooling costs because a whole house fan uses a fraction of the electricity an AC compressor does. The savings come from running your AC fewer hours each day — using the fan on cool mornings and evenings, and saving the AC for the hottest part of the afternoon. We'll give you a realistic estimate for your specific home during the free assessment.
Does a whole house fan work well in Murrieta and Temecula's climate?
Yes — the Temecula Valley is close to an ideal climate for whole house fans. We get hot days but the dry inland air cools off significantly at night, often into the 60s even after a 100°F afternoon. That day-to-night temperature swing is exactly what a whole house fan takes advantage of. Coastal homes with little overnight cooling benefit less; inland valley homes like ours benefit a lot.
Do I still need air conditioning if I install a whole house fan?
Yes, you'll still want your AC for peak afternoon heat and for the worst stretches of summer. A whole house fan and an air conditioner are a team, not competitors. The fan handles cool mornings, evenings, and milder days; the AC handles the hottest hours. Used together, your AC runs less, lasts longer, and costs less to operate.
How much does whole house fan installation cost?
Cost depends on the size of your home, the fan model, and your attic's existing ventilation. A standard single-fan installation in an average home is a straightforward one-day job; larger homes or homes that need additional attic venting cost more. We give you a written, itemized estimate before any work starts — and estimates on new installs are free. Call (951) 634-3233 for a quote.
Are modern whole house fans noisy?
Modern whole house fans are dramatically quieter than the loud attic fans many people remember from older homes. Today's systems use insulated ducting, sealed dampers, and variable-speed motors that run quietly even on high. Properly sized and installed, you'll hear a soft rush of air, not a roar. Correct sizing and clean installation are what keep them quiet — which is why installation quality matters.
Can a whole house fan be installed in any home?
Most homes are good candidates, but it depends on attic space, attic ventilation, and ceiling layout. The fan needs enough attic exhaust venting to push air out, or it can't move air efficiently. During the in-home assessment we check your attic, confirm you have — or can add — adequate venting, and recommend the right size. If a whole house fan isn't the right fit for your home, we'll tell you honestly.
Do you offer free estimates on whole house fan installation?
Yes — free, no-pressure estimates on all whole house fan installations and replacements. We'll assess your home, explain your options with real numbers, and let you decide. Call (951) 634-3233 to schedule your free in-home assessment.
