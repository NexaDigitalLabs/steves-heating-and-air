Energy Audits

Comprehensive home & business energy assessments

An energy audit is the single most cost-effective service we offer — and one most HVAC contractors don't even provide. Steve's comprehensive audit identifies exactly where your Murrieta or Temecula home is losing energy: poorly sealed ductwork (one of the biggest culprits in this market), insufficient insulation in attics and walls, drafty windows and door seals, oversized or undersized HVAC equipment that short-cycles or runs constantly, and thermal weak spots you'd never spot without proper diagnostic equipment.

You get a written report with prioritized recommendations and projected savings for each fix. Many customers recover the cost of the audit itself within the first 2-3 months of implementing our recommendations — and the changes pay back for the life of the home. We also handle commercial energy audits for businesses across the SW Riverside County region.

Common Questions

Energy Audits FAQs

Straight answers about energy audits in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

What's included in a Steve's energy audit?
Whole-home thermal imaging (finds insulation gaps and air leaks invisible to the eye), ductwork inspection (most homes lose 20-30% of conditioned air through leaks), attic and wall insulation evaluation, HVAC right-sizing analysis, window and door envelope check, and a written report with every finding prioritized by ROI. Commercial audits include the same plus building envelope analysis.
How much does a home energy audit cost?
Energy audits are paid services (not free estimates like installations), and pricing depends on home size and scope. Call (951) 634-3233 for current rates. Most customers recover the audit cost within 2-3 months of implementing the top recommendations.
How long does an energy audit take?
Typical residential audits take 2-4 hours on-site, plus another day or two to compile the written report. Larger homes or complex systems take longer. We don't rush — finding the issues that actually matter requires careful inspection.
Will the audit really pay for itself?
For the vast majority of customers, yes — and faster than they expect. Common findings: 20-30% duct leakage (huge AC bill impact), undersized return ducts (forces blower to work harder), improperly sized HVAC equipment (runs constantly or short-cycles), insufficient attic insulation. Implementing the top 2-3 recommendations typically saves enough in the first 2-3 months of summer to cover the audit cost.
Do you do commercial energy audits?
Yes — commercial audits cover building envelope, HVAC systems, lighting, and process loads. We deliver a written report with prioritized recommendations and ROI calculations. Useful for owners trying to reduce operating costs or for tenants negotiating utility allowances.
What rebates are available for energy efficiency upgrades?
Several. Federal IRA tax credits cover qualifying heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, insulation upgrades, and electrical panel upgrades. California's TECH Clean California program offers heat pump rebates (some Southern California funding currently waitlisted). SCE may offer additional incentives for whole-home electrification. We'll list every program you qualify for in the audit report.
Will the audit identify mold or air quality problems?
Often, yes. Thermal imaging picks up moisture intrusion, and our visual inspection of HVAC equipment, ducts, and building envelope frequently surfaces air quality issues — mold growth, condensation problems, ventilation deficiencies. We'll flag what we find and recommend follow-up testing if needed.
How do I schedule an energy audit?
Call (951) 634-3233 or email stevesac_6708@yahoo.com. We'll discuss your situation, give you an honest sense of whether a full audit makes sense (sometimes a targeted inspection is cheaper and equally useful), and book a time.
