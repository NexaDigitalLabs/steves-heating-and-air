HVAC Maintenance

Annual tune-ups & system health checks

The single best way to avoid a mid-summer AC emergency in 105°F Temecula heat is a proper spring tune-up. The single best way to avoid a freezing January morning with a dead furnace is a proper fall inspection. Steve's HVAC maintenance service is the boring-but-essential thing that keeps systems running for 15-20 years instead of dying at year 8 from neglect.

Our maintenance includes a comprehensive system inspection, cleaning of all critical components (coils, condenser fins, blower assembly), refrigerant level check on AC and heat pump systems, electrical connection inspection, thermostat calibration, and a full performance test. We document everything and give you a plain-language report on your system's condition — so you can plan ahead instead of reacting in panic mode when something fails.

How It Works

Our Maintenance Process

Thorough tune-ups that actually catch problems early.

1

Schedule Your Tune-Up

You call or book online, and we set up a time that works around your schedule. We confirm the system(s) in your home — AC, furnace, heat pump, mini-split — so we bring the right parts and tools the day of the visit.

2

On-Site Inspection

Our tech does a full inspection: refrigerant levels and pressures, electrical connections, capacitor health, thermostat calibration, condenser coil condition, blower motor, and condensate drainage. We check what actually fails — not a checkbox list.

3

Tune-Up & Cleaning

We clean the condenser coils, check or replace filters, tighten electrical connections, lubricate moving parts where applicable, clear the condensate line, and verify proper airflow and temperature differentials. Real work that extends equipment life.

4

System Report & Recommendations

You get an honest report of what we found — what's working well, what's wearing, and what needs attention now versus what can wait. If something needs repair, we tell you what it'll cost. No pressure to upsize or replace if your system is fine.

Common Questions

HVAC Maintenance FAQs

Straight answers about hvac maintenance in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

How often should I service my HVAC system?
Once a year minimum. Ideally: a spring AC tune-up before the first heat wave, and a fall furnace inspection before the first cold night. Heat pumps benefit from being checked twice yearly since they run year-round. Annual maintenance catches small problems before they become emergency failures.
What's included in a Steve's HVAC tune-up?
Comprehensive system inspection, cleaning of coils and critical components, refrigerant level check (AC and heat pumps), electrical connection inspection, thermostat calibration, and a full performance test. You get a written report on your system's condition with any recommendations prioritized by urgency.
How much does an annual HVAC tune-up cost?
Pricing varies based on system type and any specific concerns. Call (951) 634-3233 for current rates — and ask about scheduling a spring AC tune-up and fall furnace inspection together for valley homes.
When should I schedule my AC tune-up?
March through May, before the first heat wave hits. Once temperatures climb past 95°F regularly (typically late May or June in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley), our schedule fills up fast with both maintenance and emergency repair calls. Booking a tune-up in March or April means you get the slot you want and your AC is ready when you need it.
Why is annual HVAC maintenance important?
Three reasons: it extends equipment life dramatically (often 5-8 extra years), it improves energy efficiency (a tuned system uses 10-20% less power), and it catches problems while they're still cheap fixes instead of expensive emergencies. Skipping maintenance is one of the most expensive ways to save money.
Do I need to be home during the tune-up?
Yes — we need access to indoor units (air handler, furnace) which are usually in the garage, attic, or a closet. Most tune-ups take 60-90 minutes. We can work around your schedule, including some evening and Saturday morning slots.
How long does an HVAC tune-up take?
Typically 60-90 minutes for a single AC or furnace, longer if you have a more complex system or if we discover something that needs attention. We won't rush you, and we won't pad the job — we'll be done when the work is done right.
Do you offer maintenance plans?
Call (951) 634-3233 to discuss current maintenance plan options. Plans typically include scheduled tune-ups plus priority service if something does fail mid-season. We'll lay out the options honestly so you can pick what makes sense for your system and your situation.
