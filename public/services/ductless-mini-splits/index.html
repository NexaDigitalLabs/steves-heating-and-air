Ductless Mini-Splits

Installation, repair & multi-zone systems for ductless heating and cooling

A ductless mini-split is the answer for any space your central system was never designed to reach. Older Murrieta and Temecula homes that never had ductwork, a converted garage, a new addition, an ADU, a sunroom that bakes in the afternoon, a bedroom that's always five degrees off from the rest of the house — a mini-split handles all of it. The system is simple: a compact outdoor unit connected to one or more wall-mounted indoor heads, linked by a small line set instead of bulky ducts. Each indoor head heats and cools independently, so you only condition the rooms you're using.

Because mini-splits are heat pumps, they do both jobs — efficient cooling in summer and efficient heating in winter — from one system. They're also among the most efficient HVAC equipment available, since there's no duct loss eating into performance. We install and service single-zone systems for one problem room and multi-zone systems that can condition an entire home. We'll size the system to the actual space — an oversized mini-split short-cycles and an undersized one never keeps up — and we mount the indoor heads and route the line set cleanly so the install looks intentional, not improvised. If a mini-split isn't the right call for your situation, we'll tell you that too.

Common Questions

Ductless Mini-Splits FAQs

Straight answers about ductless mini-splits in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

What is a ductless mini-split and how does it work?
A ductless mini-split is a heat pump system with a compact outdoor unit connected to one or more wall-mounted indoor units by a small line set — no ductwork required. Each indoor unit heats and cools its room independently. It's ideal for spaces your central system can't reach well: additions, garages, ADUs, older homes without ducts, and rooms that are always too hot or too cold.
Where do mini-splits make the most sense?
Anywhere ductwork is missing, impractical, or expensive to run: older Murrieta and Temecula homes built without central air, converted garages, ADUs, new additions, sunrooms, home offices, and single bedrooms that never feel comfortable. They're also a smart way to add cooling to one area without upsizing your whole central system.
Do mini-splits heat as well as cool?
Yes. Mini-splits are heat pumps, so they do both — efficient cooling in summer and efficient heating in winter — from the same system. In our mild SoCal winters they heat very effectively and economically, which makes them a true year-round solution rather than just a cooling add-on.
How many indoor units do I need?
It depends on how many separate areas you want to control. A single-zone system has one indoor head for one room. A multi-zone system runs several indoor heads off one outdoor unit, each controlled independently — so you could condition a whole home or just the rooms you use. We'll recommend the right configuration during the assessment.
How much does mini-split installation cost?
It depends mostly on the number of zones, the system capacity, and the install complexity (wall access, electrical, line-set routing). A single-zone install is a straightforward job; multi-zone systems cost more because there's more equipment. We give you a written, itemized estimate before any work begins, and estimates on new installs are free. Call (951) 634-3233.
Are mini-splits energy efficient?
Very. Mini-splits are among the most efficient HVAC systems available, partly because there's no ductwork — central systems lose a meaningful chunk of their conditioned air to duct leakage and heat gain, and mini-splits skip that loss entirely. Zoning adds to the savings, since you're only conditioning the rooms you're actually using.
Will a mini-split look bulky on my wall?
Modern indoor heads are slim and low-profile, and a clean install makes a big difference — properly routed line sets and well-placed heads look intentional. We take care with placement and routing so the system blends in rather than standing out. We'll talk through head placement with you before we install.
Do you offer free estimates on mini-split installation?
Yes — free, no-pressure estimates on all mini-split installations and replacements. We assess the space, explain single-zone vs. multi-zone with real numbers, and let you decide. Call (951) 634-3233 to schedule your free in-home assessment.
