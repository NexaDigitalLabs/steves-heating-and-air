Residential Refrigeration FAQs

Straight answers about residential refrigeration in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

Do you install wine cellar refrigeration in Temecula homes?
Yes — Temecula and Murrieta wine cellar installs are one of our specialties. We design and install custom systems ranging from small closet conversions to full walk-in cellars with multiple temperature zones for reds and whites. Call (951) 634-3233 for a consultation.
How much does a wine cellar refrigeration system cost?
Costs vary widely based on cellar size, cooling load, and whether you want a single zone or multiple temperature zones. Small closet-style cellar systems can start around $2,000-$4,000; larger custom installs with proper insulation, vapor barriers, and multi-zone cooling can run $8,000-$20,000+. We'll give you a clear written quote after evaluating your space.
Can you repair an existing wine cooler or cellar?
Yes — we repair all major wine refrigeration brands and work with both ducted split systems and self-contained units. Common issues we see: refrigerant leaks, compressor failures, condensate problems, and temperature regulation drift. Call (951) 634-3233 with the make/model and a description of the issue.
What's the difference between residential and commercial refrigeration?
Residential systems are sized for home use — wine cellars, custom kitchens, hobbyist cold storage. They typically have lower duty cycles and quieter operation. Commercial systems are built for constant heavy use in restaurants and retail. We do both — see our commercial refrigeration page for restaurant walk-ins, freezers, and display cases.
Do you install walk-in coolers in residential homes?
Yes — residential walk-in coolers are increasingly popular for serious cooks, large families, and game/livestock processing. We handle design, refrigeration installation, and ongoing maintenance. Insulation specs, drainage, and ventilation all matter — we'll walk you through the full scope.
How long does residential refrigeration equipment last?
Properly maintained residential refrigeration systems typically last 15-20 years. Wine cellar split systems may need refrigerant top-offs every 5-7 years depending on system design. Annual maintenance extends life significantly and catches problems before they become expensive failures.
Do you handle refrigerant work on older systems?
Yes — we're EPA-certified for refrigerant recovery, recharge, and replacement. Older systems running R-22 (now phased out) can often be retrofitted with approved alternatives, though sometimes replacement is the more cost-effective path. We'll give you the honest assessment.
Do you give free estimates on residential refrigeration installs?
Yes — free in-home estimates on all installation projects. A diagnostic fee may apply for repair calls. Call (951) 634-3233 to schedule.
