Drain Cleaning

Slow drains, clogs & HVAC condensate line service

A slow or clogged drain is one of those problems that's easy to ignore until it isn't — and then it's standing water, a backed-up sink, or an overflow you're cleaning up at the worst possible time. We provide professional drain cleaning across the Murrieta-Temecula Valley for the common residential drain problems: slow sinks and tubs, stubborn clogs, and drains that keep backing up no matter how much store-bought product gets poured down them. Our technicians use proper tools and techniques to actually clear the line, not just punch a hole through the clog so it comes back next month.

There's also a drain problem specific to HVAC that a lot of homeowners don't know about until it causes damage: the condensate drain line. Your air conditioner pulls humidity out of the air, and that water has to drain somewhere. When the condensate line clogs with algae and buildup — which is common in our climate — the water backs up. That can mean a safety switch shutting your AC down on a hot day, or worse, water overflowing into a ceiling or wall. Because we're an HVAC company, we handle both sides: the everyday household drains and the condensate lines that protect your system and your home. We'll diagnose what's actually causing a recurring clog rather than just clearing it again, and we keep it honest — our focus is getting your drains working, not finding extra work.

Drain Cleaning FAQs

Straight answers about drain cleaning in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

Do you offer same-day drain cleaning?
Yes — we offer same-day appointments for most drain clogs. Call (951) 634-3233 and we'll get you on the schedule as quickly as we can. For non-urgent drain work, we offer flexible scheduling around your day.
What kinds of drains do you clear?
We handle the common residential drain problems — slow sinks, tubs, and showers, and stubborn clogs that keep backing up. As an HVAC company, we also service HVAC condensate drain lines, which is a drain problem specific to your air conditioning that many companies don't touch.
What is an HVAC condensate drain and why does it matter?
Your air conditioner removes humidity from the air, and that water drains out through the condensate line. In our climate, that line commonly clogs with algae and buildup. When it does, water backs up — which can trip a safety switch and shut your AC down on a hot day, or overflow into a ceiling or wall and cause real damage. Keeping it clear protects both your system and your home.
Why does my drain keep clogging even after I clear it?
A drain that keeps backing up usually means the underlying cause was never addressed — the clog got punched through, not cleared, or there's buildup, a partial obstruction, or a deeper issue. We focus on diagnosing the actual cause of a recurring clog rather than just clearing it one more time.
Will store-bought drain cleaner fix my clog?
Sometimes it helps with a minor clog, but for stubborn or recurring clogs it often just delays the problem — and repeated use can be hard on your pipes. If you're pouring product down a drain regularly, that's a sign the real cause needs to be diagnosed and cleared properly.
