Common Questions

Furnaces FAQs

Straight answers about furnaces in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

How much does furnace repair cost in Murrieta and Temecula?
Most furnace repairs in our area run between $200 and $700 depending on the issue. Common fixes like a failed igniter, flame sensor, or thermocouple typically land at the lower end. Inducer motor or control board replacements cost more. Heat exchanger problems usually mean replacement makes more sense than repair. We give you a written estimate before any work begins. Call (951) 634-3233 for a free quote.
Should I repair or replace my old furnace?
If your furnace is over 15 years old and the repair quote is more than half the cost of a new unit, replacement usually makes more financial sense. A modern 95%+ AFUE high-efficiency furnace can cut your gas bill 20-30% compared to an older 80% AFUE unit. We'll lay out both options with real numbers — no upsell pressure.
Why is my furnace blowing cold air?
Most common causes: thermostat is set to 'fan on' instead of 'auto', the pilot light is out (older units), the igniter has failed, or the flame sensor needs cleaning. If you see error codes flashing on the unit, write them down and call (951) 634-3233 — we can often diagnose over the phone before dispatching a tech.
Do you install all furnace fuel types?
Yes — natural gas, electric, propane, oil, and water furnaces. We also handle full retrofit projects, including converting older oil furnaces to modern gas or electric heat pump systems. Major brands serviced: Carrier, Lennox, Trane, Rheem, Goodman, York, American Standard, Bryant.
How long does a new furnace installation take?
A standard residential furnace replacement (existing ductwork, swap-out install) typically takes 4-8 hours and is completed in a single day. Full new installs including ductwork or fuel-type changes can take 1-2 days. We'll give you a clear timeline with your written quote.
What size furnace do I need for my home?
Furnace sizing isn't just about square footage — it depends on insulation quality, ductwork condition, ceiling height, window efficiency, and your local climate. Oversized furnaces short-cycle and waste fuel; undersized ones can't keep up. We do a Manual J load calculation as part of every install quote to size the unit correctly for your specific Murrieta or Temecula home.
Are there rebates available for high-efficiency furnaces in California?
Direct rebates for natural gas furnaces are limited in California right now — the state's incentive programs heavily favor heat pumps for heating. If you're considering a new furnace, ask us about whether a heat pump system makes more financial sense for your situation given the available rebates and federal tax credits.
Do you give free estimates on furnace installation?
Yes — free in-home estimates on all furnace installations, replacements, and retrofits. A diagnostic fee may apply for repair calls. Call (951) 634-3233 to schedule.
