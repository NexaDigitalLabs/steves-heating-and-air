Air Conditioning FAQs

Straight answers about AC repair, installation, and maintenance in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

How much does AC repair cost in Murrieta and Temecula?
Most AC repairs in our service area run between $185 and $650 depending on the issue. Common fixes like a failed capacitor, contactor, or fan motor typically land at the lower end. Refrigerant leak repairs and compressor work cost more. We give you a written estimate before any work begins — no surprises. Call (951) 634-3233 for a free quote.
Should I repair or replace my AC unit?
As a rule of thumb in SoCal: if your AC is over 12 years old and the repair quote is more than half the cost of a new unit, replacement usually makes more financial sense. Modern high-efficiency units can cut your summer electricity bill by 20-40%. We'll lay out both options with real numbers — no upsell pressure.
Do you offer same-day emergency AC repair?
Yes — when temperatures climb past 100°F in the Temecula Valley, an AC outage isn't just uncomfortable, it's dangerous. We provide same-day emergency response across Murrieta, Temecula, and SW Riverside County. Call (951) 634-3233 and we'll dispatch a NATE-certified tech as fast as possible.
What AC brands do you service and install?
We service and install all major brands: Carrier, Lennox, Trane, Rheem, Goodman, Daikin, York, American Standard, Bryant, and Amana. Our technicians are factory-trained on every system we touch. If you have a brand not listed, call us — we likely service it.
How often should I tune up my AC in Southern California?
Once a year, ideally in spring before the summer heat hits. SoCal AC units run hard from May through October, often through 100°F+ days. A spring tune-up catches small problems (low refrigerant, dirty coils, weak capacitors) before they become emergency failures during a heat wave.
Can you install AC in older homes without ductwork?
Yes. Ductless mini-split systems are the perfect solution for older homes, additions, garages, ADUs, and single rooms that need cooling without the cost of running new ductwork. We install Mitsubishi, Daikin, Fujitsu, and other top mini-split brands. Multi-zone systems can cool an entire home efficiently.
How long does a new central AC installation take?
A standard residential AC replacement (existing ductwork, swap-out install) typically takes 4-8 hours and is completed in a single day. Full new installations including ductwork or major upgrades can take 2-3 days. We'll give you a clear timeline with your written quote.
Do you give free estimates on AC installation?
Yes — free estimates on all AC installations and replacements. A diagnostic fee may apply for repair calls. Call (951) 634-3233 for current rates and to schedule your free in-home estimate.
