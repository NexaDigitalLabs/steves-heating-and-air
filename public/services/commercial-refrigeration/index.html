How It Works

Our Commercial Refrigeration Process

Restaurant-ready installs and reliable ongoing service.

1

Site Survey & Business Needs Assessment

We come out to your restaurant, market, or facility, look at your space and product needs, and talk through capacity, traffic patterns, and uptime requirements. Commercial refrigeration is mission-critical — we design around your operation, not a generic spec.

2

System Design & Equipment Selection

We spec the right walk-in cooler, freezer, prep table, or display case for your business. Properly sized, energy-efficient equipment from brands that hold up to commercial use. Written, itemized estimate before any work begins.

3

Professional Installation

We coordinate around your business hours to minimize disruption. Refrigeration lines, electrical, drainage, and structural work are all handled cleanly. We can also work with your general contractor on new buildouts or remodels.

4

Commissioning & Service Agreement

We test every unit under load, document temperatures, and walk you through controls and routine maintenance. Then we offer a service agreement option — because for commercial refrigeration, fast response when something fails is what protects your inventory.

Common Questions

Commercial Refrigeration FAQs

Straight answers about commercial refrigeration in the Murrieta-Temecula Valley. Don't see your question? Call (951) 634-3233.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency commercial refrigeration repair?
We offer same-day emergency response for commercial refrigeration failures during business hours and emergency dispatch for after-hours critical situations. When restaurant walk-in fails on a Friday night, you can't wait until Monday — call (951) 634-3233 and we'll get someone on it as fast as possible.
How fast can you respond to a commercial refrigeration failure?
For most locations across Murrieta, Temecula, Menifee, and the immediate valley, we can typically have a tech on-site within 2-4 hours during business hours. Customers on our preventive maintenance contracts get priority response, often same-hour. Call as soon as you notice a problem — temperature drift is the early warning.
Do you offer preventive maintenance contracts for restaurants?
Yes — monthly and quarterly maintenance contracts are available for restaurants, retail food service, and other commercial operations. Regular preventive checks dramatically reduce emergency failures and keep you compliant with health code temperature requirements. Call (951) 634-3233 for current contract pricing.
What types of commercial refrigeration do you service?
Walk-in coolers and freezers, reach-in coolers, prep tables, refrigerated display cases, ice machines, glass-door merchandisers, and specialty equipment like blast chillers. We service all major commercial brands.
Can you help with health code compliance?
Yes — temperature logs, equipment certifications, refrigerant compliance documentation, and inspector-ready repair records. If you've had a health inspector flag a temperature issue, call (951) 634-3233 and we'll get you back into compliance fast.
Can you work around our restaurant's hours?
Yes — we know restaurants run on tight schedules. We do non-emergency installations and major maintenance work during off-peak hours (mornings before lunch, late afternoon between services, full nights when needed). Tell us when you need us and we'll work around it.
Do you service commercial ice machines?
Yes — installation, repair, and preventive maintenance on commercial ice machines (Manitowoc, Hoshizaki, Scotsman, Ice-O-Matic, and others). Ice machine failures are one of the most common commercial refrigeration calls — usually scale buildup, water filtration issues, or condenser problems.
Do you give free commercial estimates?
Yes — free site survey and written estimates on all commercial installations and major projects. A diagnostic fee applies for repair calls. Call (951) 634-3233 to schedule a site visit.
