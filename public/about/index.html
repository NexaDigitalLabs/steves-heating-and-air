Who We Are

Built on Honesty.
Proven by Results.

Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating was founded by Steven M. Salvesen in 2004, built on a simple idea: give every customer the same straight answers and skilled work you'd give your own family. That philosophy has guided this business since the beginning, and it's why we still have customers who have called us for 15+ years.

We're not a franchise or a corporate chain. When you call Steve's, you get real technicians who have been in the HVAC trade for decades — people who take pride in their work and stand behind every job. No call centers, no bait-and-switch, no high-pressure sales tactics, no commissioned salespeople pushing equipment you don't need.

We hold California CSLB License #1031139 (Warm Air Heating, Ventilating and Air Conditioning), are NATE-certified (the industry's most rigorous technician certification), and are fully bonded and insured on every job. Our team has accumulated 612+ five-star Google reviews serving homeowners and businesses across Murrieta, Temecula, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, and Menifee.
