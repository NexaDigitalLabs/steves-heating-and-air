Why your AC is blowing warm air on a hot Murrieta afternoon

Explore common reasons why your AC might blow warm air during Murrieta's hot summer days and learn simple fixes.

By Steve’s Team

There's nothing quite like the frustration of feeling warm air blowing from your AC on a scorching Murrieta afternoon. When the mercury hits triple digits, you need your air conditioning to deliver cool relief, not add to the heat.

Quick Answer

Your AC might blow warm air due to thermostat issues, refrigerant leaks, or dirty filters. Check these first to restore cool air.

Check Your Thermostat Settings

Before diving into more complex solutions, ensure your thermostat is set to 'cool' and the fan is on 'auto.' It's a simple step, but it's easy to overlook. Sometimes, a switch gets bumped, especially if you have kids or pets.

Inspect Your Air Filters

Dirty air filters can restrict airflow, causing your AC to struggle and blow warm air. Check your filters monthly, especially during summer when dust and allergens are high. Replace them if they're dirty to keep air flowing smoothly.

Refrigerant Levels Might Be Low

Low refrigerant levels can lead to warm air. This isn't something you can fix yourself, but it's a common issue. If you suspect this is the case, send Steve a photo of your setup, and we can help diagnose the problem.

Check for Electrical Issues

If your AC unit isn't getting power, it won't cool your home. Check your circuit breaker to ensure it hasn't tripped. If your unit keeps losing power, it might be time to explore our services for a detailed inspection.

When to Call a Professional

If you've checked the basics and your AC is still blowing warm air, it's time to call in the pros. Our team at Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating is ready to help diagnose and fix any issues. Request a quote or schedule a service call to get your AC back on track.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my AC blowing warm air?

Common reasons include incorrect thermostat settings, dirty filters, low refrigerant levels, or electrical issues.

Can I fix my AC blowing warm air myself?

You can check and adjust thermostat settings, replace dirty filters, and check your circuit breaker. For refrigerant issues, contact a professional.

How often should I replace my air filters?

Replace your air filters every 1-3 months, more frequently during high-use periods like summer.

What if my AC still blows warm air after checking the basics?

Contact a professional to diagnose and repair more complex issues.
