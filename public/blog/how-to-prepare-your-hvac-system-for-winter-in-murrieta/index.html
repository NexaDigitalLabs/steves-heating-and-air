How to prepare your HVAC system for winter in Murrieta

Discover steps to prepare your HVAC system for Murrieta's mild winters, ensuring comfort and efficiency in your home.

By Steve’s Team • 8 min read

As the temperatures start to dip in Murrieta, it's time to make sure your HVAC system is ready for the cooler months ahead. While winters here are mild compared to other places, you still want your home to feel cozy when those chilly mornings hit.

Quick Answer

To prepare your HVAC system for winter, check your thermostat settings, replace air filters, schedule a maintenance check, and ensure vents are unobstructed.

Check your thermostat settings

As the season changes, adjust your thermostat from cooling to heating. Make sure it's programmed for energy efficiency, especially during those cool nights and mornings. Consider a smart thermostat for more precise control.

Replace or clean air filters

Dust and allergens can build up in your system over time. Before running your heat, replace or clean your air filters. This improves air quality and system efficiency, crucial for those with allergies in our dusty climate.

Clear and open vents

Blocked vents can cause uneven heating and strain your system. Walk through your home and ensure all vents are open and unobstructed by furniture or curtains.

Schedule a professional maintenance check

Regular maintenance can prevent unexpected breakdowns. Our team at Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating can perform a thorough checkup to ensure your system is running smoothly. Learn more about our maintenance services.

Inspect your ductwork

Ensure that your ducts are sealed and insulated properly. Leaky ducts can lead to energy loss and higher bills. If you're unsure, send Steve a photo for a free diagnosis.

Consider upgrading your system

If your system is over 10-15 years old, it might be time for an upgrade. Newer models are more efficient and can save you money in the long run. Explore our heating services for more options.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I replace my HVAC filter?

It's best to replace your filter every 1-3 months, especially in a dusty environment like Murrieta.

What temperature should I set my thermostat to in winter?

A comfortable range is between 68-72°F during the day and lower at night to save energy.

Is annual HVAC maintenance necessary?

Yes, annual maintenance helps ensure your system runs efficiently and can prevent costly repairs.

Can I perform HVAC maintenance myself?

While you can handle basic tasks like filter changes, professional maintenance is recommended for thorough inspections.
