Why is my AC not cooling properly and how do we fix it?

Learn common reasons your AC isn't cooling properly and how to fix them, with tips from Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating in Murrieta and Temecula.

By Steve’s Team • 8 min read

When the summer heat hits Murrieta and Temecula, the last thing you want is for your air conditioner to stop cooling properly. It’s frustrating and uncomfortable, especially when temperatures soar over 100°F. Let's look at some common reasons why your AC might not be cooling and how you can fix it.

Quick Answer

Your AC might not be cooling properly due to a dirty filter, refrigerant leak, or blocked condenser. Check these components and call a technician if needed.

Check Your Air Filter

A clogged air filter is one of the most common reasons your AC isn't cooling effectively. When the filter is dirty, it restricts airflow, making it harder for your system to circulate cool air. Check your filter monthly, especially during peak usage, and replace it if it's dirty.

Inspect the Thermostat

Sometimes, the issue might be as simple as an incorrect thermostat setting. Ensure it's set to 'cool' and the temperature is set lower than the current room temperature. If your thermostat is malfunctioning, it might need a battery change or recalibration.

Look for Refrigerant Leaks

Low refrigerant levels can severely impact your AC's ability to cool. If you suspect a leak, look for ice on the refrigerant lines or listen for a hissing sound. This is a job for a professional, so send Steve a photo or request a service call.

Check the Outdoor Unit

The outdoor condenser unit needs to be clear of debris to function properly. Leaves, dirt, and grass can block the airflow. Ensure the area around it is clean and the unit is not obstructed.

Inspect the Ductwork

Leaky ducts can lead to significant cooling loss. If you notice uneven cooling in different rooms, it might be due to leaks or blockages in the ductwork. This issue often requires a professional inspection.

When to Call a Professional

If you've checked these common issues and your AC still isn't cooling, it might be time to call in a professional. We at Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating are here to help. Whether it's a complex repair or routine maintenance, we've got you covered.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I replace my air filter?

Replace your air filter every 1-3 months, depending on usage and air quality.

What should I do if my AC is leaking water?

Check for a clogged condensate drain. If the issue persists, contact a professional.

Can I add refrigerant to my AC myself?

No, adding refrigerant requires professional equipment and expertise. Contact a technician.

Why is one room cooler than the others?

This could be due to ductwork issues or an improperly balanced system. A professional can help diagnose the problem.
