Why is my AC not cooling properly and how do we fix it?

Learn why your AC might not be cooling properly and how to fix it in Murrieta and Temecula's hot summers. Practical tips from Steve's HVAC.

By Steve’s Team

There's nothing worse than flipping on your AC during a scorching Murrieta summer afternoon only to find it's not cooling properly. If you've ever wondered, "Why is my AC not cooling properly and how do we fix it?" you're not alone. Let's dive into some common reasons and solutions.

Quick Answer

Your AC might not be cooling properly due to issues like dirty filters, refrigerant leaks, or thermostat problems. Regular maintenance can often fix these issues.

Common Reasons Your AC Isn't Cooling

Dirty Air Filters

One of the most common culprits is a dirty air filter. When filters get clogged, airflow is restricted, making it harder for your system to cool your home. Check your filters monthly, especially during high-use periods.

Refrigerant Leaks

Refrigerant is essential for cooling. If there's a leak, your AC won't be able to cool effectively. A professional can diagnose and repair leaks, ensuring your system is charged correctly.

Thermostat Issues

Sometimes, the problem is as simple as a malfunctioning thermostat. Make sure it's set to "cool" and the temperature is set lower than the current room temperature. If it still doesn't work, it might need recalibration or replacement.

Blocked Condenser Unit

The outdoor condenser unit needs to be clear of debris and obstructions. Leaves, dirt, and other debris can block airflow, reducing efficiency. Check it regularly and keep the area around it clean.

Electrical Problems

Tripped breakers or blown fuses can also cause your AC to stop cooling. If your unit isn't turning on at all, check your electrical panel. If it keeps happening, call a professional to inspect your system.

How to Fix These Issues

Many of these problems can be prevented or fixed with regular maintenance. Here's what you can do:

  • Replace air filters: Do this every 1-3 months depending on usage and filter type.
  • Check and clean the condenser: Keep the area around your outdoor unit clear.
  • Schedule regular maintenance: Professional tune-ups can catch issues before they become major problems.
  • Check your thermostat: Ensure it's functioning properly and replace batteries if needed.

If these steps don't solve the problem, it might be time to call in the professionals. At Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating, we offer comprehensive AC repair services to get your system back in top shape.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if my AC is still not cooling after checking these issues?

If your AC still isn't cooling, it might be time to contact a professional for a thorough inspection. There could be deeper issues that need expert attention.

How often should I schedule AC maintenance?

It's recommended to have your AC serviced at least once a year, ideally before the hot summer months.

Can I fix a refrigerant leak myself?

Refrigerant leaks should be handled by professionals. They have the tools and knowledge to safely repair and recharge your system.

Is it normal for my AC to run constantly?

In extremely hot weather, your AC might run more frequently. However, if it runs constantly without cooling effectively, it could indicate a problem.
