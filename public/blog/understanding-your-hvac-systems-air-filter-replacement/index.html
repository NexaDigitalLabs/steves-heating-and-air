Understanding your HVAC system's air filter replacement

Learn when and how to replace your HVAC air filter to improve efficiency and air quality in your Murrieta or Temecula home.

By Steve’s Team

Changing your HVAC system's air filter is one of the simplest yet most important maintenance tasks you can do to keep your system running smoothly. In Murrieta and Temecula, where dust and allergens can be a concern, regular filter changes are crucial.

Quick Answer

Replace your HVAC air filter every 1-3 months to maintain efficiency and air quality.

Why is air filter replacement important?

Your HVAC system's air filter traps dust, pollen, and other particles, preventing them from circulating in your home. A clean filter improves air quality and helps your system run efficiently.

How often should you replace your air filter?

Generally, it's recommended to replace your air filter every 1-3 months. However, factors like pets, allergies, and local dust levels may require more frequent changes.

Signs it's time to replace your air filter

  • Visible dirt or dust on the filter
  • Increased dust in your home
  • Higher energy bills
  • Reduced airflow from vents

How to replace your HVAC air filter

  1. Turn off your HVAC system.
  2. Locate the filter compartment, usually near the return air duct.
  3. Remove the old filter and note its size.
  4. Insert the new filter, ensuring the airflow arrows point in the correct direction.
  5. Turn your system back on and check for proper airflow.

Choosing the right air filter

Filters come in various types and sizes. Consider factors like MERV rating, which indicates filtration efficiency, and choose a filter that balances air quality with system performance.

Maintaining your HVAC system

Regular maintenance, including filter changes, ensures your HVAC system operates efficiently. Consider scheduling a professional check-up with Steve's HVAC Maintenance services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I clean and reuse my air filter?

Most residential HVAC filters are disposable. Reusable filters exist but require regular cleaning to remain effective.

What happens if I don't change my air filter?

A clogged filter can reduce airflow, strain your system, and lead to higher energy bills and potential damage.

How do I know what size filter I need?

Check the size printed on your current filter or consult your HVAC system's manual for the correct dimensions.
