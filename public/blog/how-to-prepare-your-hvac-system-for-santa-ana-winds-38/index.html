How to prepare your HVAC system for Santa Ana winds

Learn essential tips to protect your HVAC system during Santa Ana winds in Murrieta and Temecula. Stay prepared and avoid costly repairs.

By Steve’s Team

Living in Murrieta and Temecula, you're no stranger to the infamous Santa Ana winds. These hot, dry gusts can wreak havoc on your home, especially your HVAC system. Preparing your system for these winds can save you from unexpected breakdowns and costly repairs.

Quick Answer

Protect your HVAC system from Santa Ana winds by securing outdoor units, changing filters, and scheduling a maintenance check.

Secure Your Outdoor Unit

The first step in preparing for Santa Ana winds is to ensure your outdoor HVAC unit is secure. Check that it's anchored properly and that no debris can easily get lodged in it. This prevents damage from flying objects during strong gusts.

Change Your Air Filters

Dust and debris are common during Santa Ana winds, which can quickly clog your air filters. Make it a habit to check and replace your filters before and after the winds to maintain good indoor air quality.

Schedule a Maintenance Check

Regular maintenance is crucial, especially before the winds hit. A professional can inspect your system for any vulnerabilities and ensure it's running efficiently. Explore our maintenance services to keep your system in top shape.

Clear the Surrounding Area

Make sure the area around your outdoor unit is clear of loose items like patio furniture or garden tools. These can become projectiles in high winds and damage your unit.

Consider Installing a Protective Cover

If you experience frequent strong winds, a protective cover for your outdoor unit can be a worthwhile investment. It can help shield your system from dust and debris.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I change my air filters during Santa Ana winds?

Check your filters weekly during the winds and replace them as needed to ensure optimal performance.

Can I install a protective cover myself?

Yes, many covers are designed for easy DIY installation, but you can also request professional help.

What should I do if my HVAC system is damaged during the winds?

Contact a professional immediately to assess and repair any damage. You can send Steve a photo for a quick diagnosis.
