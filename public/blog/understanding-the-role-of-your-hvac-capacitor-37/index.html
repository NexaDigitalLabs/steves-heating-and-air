Understanding the role of your HVAC capacitor

Learn how an HVAC capacitor functions and why it's crucial for your system's performance in Murrieta and Temecula.

By Steve’s Team

Your HVAC system is like a finely tuned orchestra, with each component playing a critical role in keeping your home comfortable. One of the unsung heroes of this ensemble is the capacitor. If you've ever wondered what this small but mighty part does, you're in the right place.

Quick Answer

The HVAC capacitor stores and releases energy to start and run your system's motors, ensuring efficient operation.

What is an HVAC capacitor?

A capacitor is an electrical component that stores and releases energy. In your HVAC system, it provides the initial jolt of electricity needed to start the motor and keeps it running smoothly. Think of it as the spark that ignites the engine.

Types of capacitors

There are two main types of capacitors in HVAC systems:

  • Start Capacitors: These provide the extra power needed to start the motor.
  • Run Capacitors: These help maintain a constant voltage supply to keep the motor running efficiently.

Signs of a failing capacitor

If your HVAC system is struggling to start or isn't running as efficiently as it should, a faulty capacitor might be to blame. Here are some signs:

  • The system struggles to start or won't start at all.
  • You hear a humming noise from the unit.
  • The system turns off on its own.
  • There's a noticeable drop in performance.

Why capacitor issues matter in Murrieta and Temecula

During those hot Murrieta and Temecula summers, your AC works overtime. A failing capacitor can lead to inefficient cooling or even a complete system shutdown, leaving you sweltering in the heat.

Replacing a capacitor

Replacing a capacitor is a job best left to professionals. Capacitors hold a charge even when the power is off, posing a risk of electric shock. If you suspect a capacitor issue, it's time to send Steve a photo for a quick diagnosis.

Preventive maintenance

Regular maintenance can help catch capacitor issues before they become major problems. Consider scheduling an HVAC maintenance check to ensure everything is in top shape.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an HVAC capacitor last?

Typically, capacitors last about 10-20 years, but factors like usage and climate can affect their lifespan.

Can I replace a capacitor myself?

It's not recommended due to the risk of electric shock. It's best to have a professional handle it.

What happens if a capacitor fails?

If a capacitor fails, your HVAC system may not start or run efficiently, leading to increased energy bills and discomfort.
