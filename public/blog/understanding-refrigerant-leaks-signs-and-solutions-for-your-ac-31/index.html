Understanding refrigerant leaks: Signs and solutions for your AC

Learn how to identify and address refrigerant leaks in your AC system to ensure efficient cooling during Murrieta's hot summers.

By Steve’s Team

When your air conditioner isn't cooling as it should, especially during those scorching 100°F+ summer afternoons in Murrieta, a refrigerant leak might be to blame. Understanding the signs and solutions can help you keep your cool.

Quick Answer

Refrigerant leaks can cause inefficient cooling and higher energy bills. Look for hissing sounds, frozen coils, and warm air from vents as signs.

What is refrigerant and why is it important?

Refrigerant is a crucial component of your AC system, responsible for absorbing heat from your home and releasing it outside. Without the right amount of refrigerant, your AC can't effectively cool your home.

Signs of a refrigerant leak

Here are some common indicators that your AC might be losing refrigerant:

  • Hissing or bubbling noises: These sounds can indicate a leak in the refrigerant lines.
  • Warm air from vents: If your AC is blowing warm air, it might not have enough refrigerant to cool it down.
  • Frozen evaporator coils: Low refrigerant levels can cause the coils to freeze, leading to reduced efficiency.
  • Increased energy bills: An AC with a refrigerant leak works harder to cool your home, using more energy.

How to address refrigerant leaks

If you suspect a refrigerant leak, it's important to act quickly. Here's what you can do:

  1. Turn off your AC: Running an AC with a refrigerant leak can cause further damage.
  2. Contact a professional: A licensed technician can locate and repair the leak, then recharge your system with the correct amount of refrigerant.
  3. Regular maintenance: Schedule regular check-ups to catch potential issues early. Explore our maintenance services for more information.

Preventing future leaks

Prevention is always better than cure. Regular maintenance can help prevent refrigerant leaks by ensuring that your system is in top shape. Consider our air conditioning services to keep your system running smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should refrigerant levels be checked?

Refrigerant levels should be checked during regular maintenance visits, typically once a year.

Can I add refrigerant myself?

It's not recommended. Handling refrigerant requires specialized knowledge and tools, and should be done by a professional.

What causes refrigerant leaks?

Leaks can occur due to corrosion, wear and tear, or faulty installation.

Is a refrigerant leak dangerous?

While not immediately dangerous, refrigerant leaks can lead to inefficient cooling and higher energy bills.
