Understanding refrigerant leaks and their impact on your AC system

Learn how refrigerant leaks affect your AC's efficiency and what signs to watch for in Murrieta's hot summers.

By Steve’s Team

When your air conditioner isn't cooling like it should during those scorching Murrieta summers, a refrigerant leak might be to blame. Understanding the impact of these leaks can help you maintain a comfortable home and avoid costly repairs.

Quick Answer

Refrigerant leaks reduce your AC's cooling efficiency and can cause system damage if not addressed promptly.

What is refrigerant and why is it important?

Refrigerant is the substance in your AC system that absorbs heat from your home and releases it outside, keeping your indoor environment cool and comfortable. Without the right amount of refrigerant, your AC can't effectively cool your home.

Signs of a refrigerant leak

Here are some common signs that your AC might be leaking refrigerant:

  • AC running constantly but not cooling effectively
  • Hissing or bubbling noises from the AC unit
  • Ice buildup on the refrigerant lines or evaporator coil
  • Higher than normal energy bills

How refrigerant leaks affect your AC system

When refrigerant levels are low, your AC has to work harder to cool your home, leading to increased wear and tear on the system. This can result in more frequent breakdowns and a shorter lifespan for your unit. Additionally, low refrigerant levels can cause the evaporator coil to freeze, further reducing efficiency.

What to do if you suspect a refrigerant leak

If you notice any signs of a refrigerant leak, it's important to contact a professional technician to diagnose and repair the issue. Attempting to fix a refrigerant leak on your own can be dangerous and may void your warranty.

Preventing refrigerant leaks

Regular maintenance is key to preventing refrigerant leaks. Scheduling annual check-ups with a trusted HVAC professional can help catch potential issues before they become major problems. Consider our HVAC maintenance services to keep your system in top shape.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add refrigerant to my AC myself?

No, adding refrigerant requires specialized tools and knowledge. It's best left to professionals.

How often should I have my AC system checked for leaks?

It's recommended to have your system checked annually as part of regular maintenance.

What happens if a refrigerant leak is not fixed?

Ignoring a refrigerant leak can lead to decreased efficiency, higher energy bills, and potential system damage.
