How to prepare your AC for the first heat wave of summer

Get your AC ready for the first summer heat wave in Murrieta and Temecula with these simple steps to ensure cool comfort.

By Steve’s Team

As summer approaches in Murrieta and Temecula, it's time to make sure your air conditioning system is ready to handle those 100°F+ afternoons. A little preparation now can keep your home cool and comfortable all season long.

Quick Answer

Check and clean your AC unit, replace filters, and ensure proper airflow to prepare for the first summer heat wave.

Check and Clean the Outdoor Unit

Start by inspecting your outdoor unit. Clear any debris like leaves or twigs that may have accumulated around it. This ensures proper airflow and efficiency.

Replace or Clean Air Filters

Dirty filters can restrict airflow and reduce efficiency. Replace or clean them before the summer heat hits. This is a simple step that can make a big difference.

Inspect Ductwork for Leaks

Leaky ducts can lead to significant energy loss. Check your ductwork for any visible leaks and seal them to improve efficiency. Our ductwork services can help if you need professional assistance.

Test Your Thermostat

Ensure your thermostat is working correctly. Consider upgrading to a programmable model for better energy management during peak heat hours.

Schedule a Professional Tune-Up

Consider scheduling a professional tune-up to ensure your system is in top shape. Our HVAC maintenance services can help catch any potential issues early.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my AC not cooling properly?

Check for dirty filters, blocked vents, or refrigerant issues. A professional inspection may be needed.

How often should I replace my air filters?

Replace or clean air filters every 1-3 months, especially during peak usage.

What temperature should I set my thermostat in summer?

Set your thermostat to 78°F when you're home for optimal energy savings and comfort.
