How often should I replace my AC filter in Murrieta?

Learn how often you should replace your AC filter in Murrieta to keep your system running efficiently and improve indoor air quality.

By Steve’s Team

If you live in Murrieta, you know how important it is to keep your air conditioning running smoothly, especially during those scorching summer days. One of the simplest yet most effective ways to maintain your AC system is by regularly replacing the air filter.

Quick Answer

Replace your AC filter every 1-3 months, depending on usage and indoor air quality needs.

Why replacing your AC filter matters

Your AC filter plays a crucial role in keeping your system efficient and your indoor air clean. It traps dust, pet dander, and other particles, preventing them from circulating through your home. A clogged filter can lead to reduced airflow, making your system work harder and increasing your energy bills.

Factors influencing filter replacement frequency

Usage and household size

If your AC runs frequently, especially during Murrieta's hot summers, you'll need to replace the filter more often. Larger households with pets or allergy sufferers might also require more frequent changes.

Filter type

Different filters have varying lifespans. Basic fiberglass filters need replacing every month, while higher-quality pleated filters can last up to three months. Consider your needs and choose a filter that balances cost and performance.

Signs it's time to replace your filter

  • Increased dust on surfaces
  • Higher energy bills
  • Reduced airflow from vents
  • Allergy symptoms worsening

If you notice any of these signs, it's likely time for a new filter.

Steps to replace your AC filter

  1. Turn off your AC system.
  2. Locate the filter compartment, typically near the return air duct.
  3. Remove the old filter and dispose of it properly.
  4. Insert the new filter, ensuring the airflow arrows are pointing in the correct direction.
  5. Turn your AC system back on.

Get professional help if needed

If you're unsure about replacing your filter or have other AC concerns, our team at Steve's Air Conditioning & Heating is here to help. We offer maintenance services to keep your system in top shape.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I clean and reuse my AC filter?

Most AC filters are designed for one-time use and should be replaced, not cleaned.

What happens if I don't replace my AC filter?

Neglecting to replace your filter can lead to reduced efficiency, higher energy bills, and potential system damage.

Where can I buy replacement filters?

You can purchase filters at local hardware stores or online. Ensure you know the correct size for your system.
