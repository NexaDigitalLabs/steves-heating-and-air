How often should I replace my AC filter in Murrieta?

Learn how often to replace your AC filter in Murrieta to keep your system efficient and your air clean. Local tips for Southwest Riverside County.

By Steve’s Team • 8 min read

Keeping your air conditioning system in top shape is crucial, especially during those scorching 100°F+ summer afternoons in Murrieta. One simple yet vital task is replacing your AC filter regularly. But how often should you really be doing it?

Quick Answer

In Murrieta, it's best to replace your AC filter every 1-3 months, depending on usage and local conditions.

Why regular filter replacement matters

Your AC filter plays a key role in maintaining both your system's efficiency and your home's air quality. A clogged filter can lead to higher energy bills, reduced cooling, and increased wear on your system.

Factors affecting replacement frequency

Usage

If your AC runs frequently, especially during the summer, you'll need to replace the filter more often. For most homes in Murrieta, every 1-2 months is a good rule of thumb during peak usage.

Pets

Homes with pets should consider more frequent changes. Pet hair and dander can quickly clog filters, so monthly replacements might be necessary.

Allergies

If anyone in your household suffers from allergies, changing the filter every month can help reduce dust and allergens.

Types of filters

Not all filters are created equal. Here are some common types:

  • Fiberglass filters: Cheap but need frequent replacement.
  • Pleated filters: Better at trapping particles, last longer.
  • HEPA filters: Best for allergies, but check compatibility with your system.

Signs it's time to change your filter

Besides a regular schedule, look out for these signs:

  • Increased dust in your home
  • Higher energy bills
  • Reduced airflow from vents

How to replace your AC filter

Replacing your filter is a straightforward task. First, turn off your system. Locate the filter slot, usually near the return vent or the air handler. Slide out the old filter and insert the new one, ensuring the arrows on the filter frame point in the direction of airflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I clean and reuse my AC filter?

Most disposable filters should not be reused. Some filters are washable, but check the manufacturer's instructions.

What happens if I don't change my filter?

Neglecting filter changes can lead to reduced efficiency, higher energy costs, and potential damage to your AC system.

Where can I buy replacement filters?

You can find them at local hardware stores or order online. Make sure to get the right size for your system.

How do I know what size filter to buy?

The size is usually printed on the side of your current filter. If not, measure the dimensions of the filter slot.
