Should I replace my old AC unit or repair it in Wildomar?

Deciding whether to replace or repair your old AC unit in Wildomar? Consider costs, efficiency, and local climate to make the best choice.

By Steve’s Team • 8 min read

If you're sweating through another sweltering summer in Wildomar, you might be wondering whether it's time to replace your old AC unit or just repair it. With temperatures regularly soaring past 100°F, a reliable air conditioner is a must. Let's break down the factors you should consider when making this decision.

Quick Answer

If your AC unit is over 10-15 years old, frequently breaks down, or your energy bills are skyrocketing, it's likely time to consider a replacement.

Age of Your AC Unit

Most air conditioning units last between 10 to 15 years. If yours is nearing or past this age, replacement might be more cost-effective in the long run. Older units are less efficient and more prone to breakdowns.

Cost of Repairs vs. Replacement

Consider the cost of repairs. A good rule of thumb is if a repair costs more than half the price of a new unit, replacement is the better option. Frequent repairs are also a sign that your unit is on its last legs.

Energy Efficiency

Older units are generally less energy-efficient. Newer models can significantly reduce your energy bills, especially during those hot Wildomar summers. Check the SEER rating of your current unit and compare it to newer models.

Performance and Comfort

If your home isn't staying cool or your unit is running constantly, it might be time for an upgrade. Newer systems not only cool better but also offer improved humidity control and quieter operation.

Environmental Impact

Older AC units use refrigerants like R-22, which are harmful to the environment. Newer units use more eco-friendly refrigerants and are better for the planet.

Local Climate Considerations

In Wildomar, with its hot, dry summers, you need an AC unit that can handle the heat. A new, efficient unit can make a big difference in comfort and cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my AC unit is too old?

If your unit is over 10-15 years old, it's likely nearing the end of its lifespan.

What are signs that my AC needs replacing?

Frequent breakdowns, high energy bills, and poor cooling are key indicators.

Is it worth repairing an old AC unit?

If repairs cost more than half of a new unit, replacement is usually better.

How can I improve my home's energy efficiency?

Consider upgrading to a high-efficiency AC unit and seal any air leaks.
