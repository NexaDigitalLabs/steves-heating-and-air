What to expect when installing a new AC system in Murrieta

Learn what to expect when installing a new AC system in Murrieta, from initial consultation to final inspection, tailored for local climate.

By Steve’s Team

Installing a new AC system in your Murrieta home is a significant investment in comfort, especially with our hot, dry summers. Knowing what to expect during the process can ease any concerns and help you prepare for the installation day.

Quick Answer

Expect a detailed consultation, professional installation, and thorough testing when installing a new AC system in Murrieta.

Initial Consultation

The first step in installing a new AC system is a detailed consultation. A professional technician will assess your home’s size, layout, and specific cooling needs. This ensures that the system installed is the right fit for your home, providing efficient cooling during those 100°F+ summer afternoons.

Choosing the Right System

Based on the consultation, you'll receive recommendations on the best AC systems suited for your home. This includes considerations for energy efficiency, budget, and any specific features you might want, such as smart thermostats or zoning capabilities.

Preparing for Installation Day

Once you've chosen your system, you'll schedule an installation day. Before the technicians arrive, clear the area around your existing AC unit and ensure easy access to the installation site. This helps the process go smoothly and quickly.

The Installation Process

On installation day, technicians will remove your old unit and set up the new system. This involves connecting the indoor and outdoor units, setting up the thermostat, and ensuring all components are properly installed. The process usually takes a full day, but it can vary based on your home's specific needs.

Testing and Final Inspection

After installation, technicians will thoroughly test the system to ensure everything is working correctly. They'll check the airflow, temperature control, and system efficiency. A final inspection ensures that your new AC system is ready to keep your home comfortable.

Post-Installation Tips

Once your new system is up and running, there are a few things you can do to maintain it. Regular maintenance, such as changing air filters and scheduling annual check-ups, will keep your system running efficiently. Consider our HVAC maintenance services to keep your system in top shape.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the installation process take?

Typically, a new AC system installation takes about one day, but it can vary depending on your home's specific requirements.

Do I need to be home during the installation?

Yes, it's best to be home to answer any questions and ensure the installation goes smoothly.

What should I do if I encounter issues after installation?

If you experience any problems, contact us immediately for assistance. We stand by our work and are here to help.

Can I install the AC system myself?

It's not recommended. Professional installation ensures safety and efficiency, and it complies with local codes.
