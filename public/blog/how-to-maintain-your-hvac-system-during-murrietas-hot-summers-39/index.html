How to maintain your HVAC system during Murrieta's hot summers

Learn essential HVAC maintenance tips to keep your system running smoothly during Murrieta's scorching summer days.

By Steve’s Team

Summers in Murrieta can be brutally hot, with temperatures often soaring above 100°F. Keeping your HVAC system in top shape is crucial to ensure comfort and efficiency during these sweltering months. Here are some straightforward tips to help you maintain your HVAC system and keep your cool all summer long.

Quick Answer

Regularly check and replace air filters, clean around the outdoor unit, and schedule professional maintenance to keep your HVAC system efficient during Murrieta's summer.

Check and Replace Air Filters Regularly

One of the simplest yet most effective maintenance tasks is checking your air filters. During the summer, your HVAC system works overtime, and clean filters are essential for optimal airflow. Check your filters monthly and replace them if they appear dirty or clogged.

Keep the Outdoor Unit Clear

Ensure that the area around your outdoor unit is free from debris, leaves, and overgrown plants. This allows for proper airflow and prevents the unit from overheating. A clear space of at least two feet around the unit is ideal.

Inspect and Clean the Condenser Coils

Condenser coils can accumulate dirt and dust over time, reducing efficiency. Carefully clean the coils with a garden hose, but be gentle to avoid damaging the fins. If you're not comfortable doing this yourself, consider hiring a professional.

Schedule Professional Maintenance

It's wise to schedule a professional HVAC maintenance visit at least once a year. A technician can perform a thorough inspection, clean components, and address any potential issues before they become costly repairs. Explore our maintenance services for more details.

Check the Thermostat Settings

Ensure your thermostat is set to the correct temperature. Consider upgrading to a programmable thermostat to optimize your cooling schedule and save on energy bills. This can be particularly useful during peak afternoon heat.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I replace my HVAC air filter in the summer?

Check your air filter monthly and replace it if it's dirty, especially during high usage periods like summer.

Why is professional maintenance important?

Professional maintenance ensures your HVAC system runs efficiently and can prevent costly repairs by catching issues early.

Can I clean the condenser coils myself?

Yes, but be gentle. Use a garden hose to rinse off dirt without bending the fins. If unsure, hire a professional.
